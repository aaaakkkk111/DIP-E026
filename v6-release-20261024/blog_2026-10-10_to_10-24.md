# Two-week progress: 2026-10-10 → 10-24 / 两周进展：2026-10-10 → 10-24

Project: adaptive control for a two-wheel self-balancing car (STM32F103, factory cascade PID + LLM-assisted gain scheduling).

## English

**Week 1 (10-10 → 10-17): motor dead-band compensation**
- Found that the motor dead band (the PWM a motor needs before it turns) is not constant: it drifts with battery voltage, temperature and wear.
- Designed a compensation module:
  - voltage feed-forward;
  - a wheels-in-the-air self-calibration;
  - a small-command hold that avoids crossing the gear backlash.
- The car was not available, so the module was built and checked in the digital twin. In the twin, a dead band 100 counts too high made the factory setting fall 6 times; with the compensation it fell 0 times. A dead band 80 counts too low gave 5× less chatter.
- An on-line estimator was tried and dropped: it was unreliable in closed loop.
- Prepared a ready-to-run real-car test plan. The module ships switched off.

**Week 2 (10-18 → 10-24): extreme cases, real geometry, and loads**
- **Large-tilt recovery.** The "40° can't be recovered" limit turned out to be the firmware's motor cut-off, not the motors. With a 45° cut-off, and the speed-loop integral held at zero during the lift, the car rights itself from 40° (4/4 in the twin). Daily driving scores were unchanged.
- **Get-up from the floor.**
  - Made the manufacturer's STEP CAD model the single source for geometry. The real contact angle is 78.5°, not the 44.5° a box model gave.
  - Designed a push–coast–push get-up: 8/8 at 12 V and at 10.5 V, including when the car is started while lying down.
  - Added a safety lock, so a fallen car never keeps spinning its wheels.
  - Mapped the physical limit of catching a falling car versus fall speed.
  - Released as v5.
- **Fitting in 64 KB of flash.** Removed every unused mode while keeping Bluetooth and the serial console.
- **Loads on the real car.**
  - Real-car tests showed a visible sway that grew until the car fell when a load sat on the top plate.
  - Traced it to the load's high centre of mass (0.15–0.25 m above the axle).
  - Proposed the rule that became v6: low-frequency sway → stiffer gear, high-frequency chatter → softer gear, calm → no change.
  - A gain sweep in the twin showed that above 0.25 m the speed loop, not the balance gains, is the limit.
- **Real-car iteration in one day.** Four test builds on 10-24:
  1. separated sway from chatter, which were fighting each other;
  2. fixed a gear that stuck after removing a load;
  3. added fast removal of small high-frequency oscillation.
  The last build was declared final as **v6**.
- **Documentation.** Packaged v6 like the v4 release: firmware, a bilingual technical README (design, principles, code walkthrough, results with factory baselines), a real-car test plan, source snapshot and twin logs. Also drafted the project's sections for the DIP final report.

## 中文

**第一周（10-10 → 10-17）：电机死区补偿**
- 发现电机死区（电机开始转动所需的 PWM）不是常数，会随电池电压、温度和磨损漂移。
- 设计了补偿模块：
  - 电压前馈；
  - 车轮离地自标定；
  - 小指令保持，避免反复穿过齿轮齿隙。
- 这段时间没有车，所以在数字孪生里完成并验证。孪生里，死区偏高 100 时原厂设置摔倒 6 次，补偿后 0 次；死区偏低 80 时抖动降到 1/5。
- 尝试过在线估计，但它在闭环里不可靠，最终放弃。
- 写好了拿到车就能直接做的实车测试计划。模块默认关闭发布。

**第二周（10-18 → 10-24）：极端情况、真实几何、负重**
- **大角度回正**：发现「40° 救不回来」是固件切电机角造成的，不是电机能力不够。切电机角改到 45°，抬车期间把速度环积分保持为 0，车能从 40° 自己回正（孪生 4/4），日常行驶跑分不变。
- **倒地自起**：
  - 以厂家 STEP 三维模型作为几何的唯一依据：真实碰地角是 78.5°，不是长方体模型算出的 44.5°。
  - 设计「推—滑行—推」自起，12 V 和 10.5 V 都是 8/8，躺着启动也能起来。
  - 加了保护锁，倒地后轮子不会一直空转。
  - 测出不同倒下速度下能接住的物理极限。
  - 发布为 v5。
- **塞进 64 KB Flash**：删掉所有用不到的模式，保留蓝牙和串口控制台。
- **实车负重**：
  - 实车测试发现负重放在顶板上时，车身摆动肉眼可见、越来越大，直到摔倒。
  - 定位到原因是负重重心高（轮轴上方 0.15–0.25 m）。
  - 提出后来成为 v6 核心的规则：低频摆动 → 升档，高频抖动 → 降档，平稳 → 不换。
  - 孪生增益扫描发现：重心 0.25 m 以上，真正的限制是速度环，而不是平衡增益。
- **一天内的实车迭代**：10-24 做了四个测试版：
  1. 把互相打架的摆动和抖动分开；
  2. 修复取下负载后档位卡住的问题；
  3. 加入小幅高频振荡的快速消除。
  最后一版定为最终版 **v6**。
- **文档**：仿照 v4 正式版整理 v6，包括固件、中英双语技术说明（设计、原理、代码讲解、带原厂基线的结果）、实车测试计划、源码快照和孪生日志。另外起草了 DIP 期末报告里的项目部分。
