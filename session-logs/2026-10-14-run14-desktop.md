## 3. Why: the pushes are partly unsurvivable

Training pushes are uniform 0–4 N for 0.05–0.15 s, so the impulse runs up to
0.6 N·s. The stock PID survives 3 N × 0.1 s (0.30 N·s) in 17 of 18 trials and
4 N × 0.1 s (0.40 N·s) in none. So the calm controller's limit is about
0.3–0.4 N·s, and 23 % of the training pushes are above 0.30 N·s, 15 % above
0.35 and 9 % above 0.40.

Each of those ends in −10 and a lost episode. The only way the policy can
survive more of them is to react harder, which the wobble penalty then
fights. Run 13 (penalty 0.5) settled on harder; run 14 (1.5) settled in
between and does neither well. A penalty weight alone won't give both.
