# 2026-10-21 — Run 20 (osc and lean inputs), desktop; run 21 started

All results are **simulator** results (MuJoCo, 2° slack). Nothing from run 20 has
been on the car. It also can't be: the firmware doesn't compute `osc` and `lean` yet
(see the run 19 log for the change).

**Settings:** run 19's settings (payload 0–2 kg, PWM-step penalty and wobble
penalty gated by tilt), plus two network inputs in the dropped wheel-angle slots
(`obs[5]`, `obs[6]`):
- `osc/25`: 8–16 Hz RMS of the pitch gyro;
- `lean/2`: 0.3–2 Hz RMS of the pitch angle.

Both are computed exactly as a teammate's Mode 26 `load_adapt.c` does
(`load_adapt_step()`). All five coefficients were checked against
α = dt/(RC + dt). A 7 Hz shake reads the same as a 12 Hz one (7.7 against 8.0), so
the band covers our 7–10 Hz limit cycle.

**Stopped at 20M of 30M** under the user's cutoff rule. The empty-car wobble rose at
every checkpoint from 16M, and 3 N empty pushes stayed at 0/18.

**Files:**
- Best-by-eval model in `models/best_real_RUN20/`
- Checkpoints in `models/ckpt_run20/`
- Progress in `train_real_robot_RUN20_osc_lean_progress.csv`

## Verdict

- **Load awareness halved the empty-car shake and delayed it by about 4M steps**, but
  didn't stop it. By 20M it was back at run 15's level.
- **Loaded, it is as good as run 19:** 5–7 deg/s standing and 18/18 pushes at 3 N.
- **The best compromise is the 15M or 16M checkpoint:** empty about 6–14 deg/s, 2 kg
  about 4–6.

## Why the shake keeps coming back (reasoning, not a controlled test)

- A 20 deg/s shake cost about 0.18 per step, against +2 for staying up and −10 per fall.
- With payload and shoves in play, the optimiser buys safety margin with stiffness and
  pays the small shake charge.
- Run 14 raised the broadband pitch-rate charge and lost pushes, because push
  recovery is large, slow motion that the same charge punishes.

## Run 21 (started)

Run 21 is run 20 plus a reward charge on the shake band only:
`−min((osc_true / 10)², 1.0)`, where `osc_true` is the `load_adapt.c` `osc` of the
true pitch gyro. It is not gated by tilt.

**Measured on the 2° test car:**

| policy | wobble (deg/s) | `osc` | charge per step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Run 20 at 11M | 3–5 | 1.4–1.7 | 0.03–0.05 |
| Run 20 at 20M | 18–33 | 8.5–18.6 | 0.94–2.0 before the cap |
| Run 15 | 18–25 | 9.4–13.4 | 0.94–1.65 before the cap |

**Cap at 1.0, not 2.0:** at 2.0 a shaking policy would earn about zero per step, and
falling on purpose could pay.

**If run 21 also fails:** stop tuning rewards. Move to a residual design, with the
stock PID or the teammate's gain-scheduled PID doing the balancing and the network
adding corrections on top.
