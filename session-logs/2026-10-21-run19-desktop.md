# 2026-10-21 — Run 19 (PWM-step penalty gated by tilt, payload 0–2 kg), desktop

All results here are **simulator** results (MuJoCo, 2° slack unless noted). Nothing from
run 19 has been on the car.

**Settings:** run 17's settings, with these changes:
- Payload 0–2 kg (`MAX_PAYLOAD_KG = 2.0`), because the car must carry about 2 kg.
- The reversal penalty is replaced by `PWM_STEP_WEIGHT = 0.3`. It charges the squared
  change in the PWM actually sent, after the +1300 compensation, scaled by 1/2800.
- That penalty and the wobble penalty are multiplied by
  `upright_gate = max(0, 1 − |θ|/0.15 rad)`, which is full at upright and zero beyond
  about 8.6°.
- A checkpoint is saved every 1M steps (`models/ckpt_run19/`).

**Stopped at 20M of 30M** under the user's cutoff rule ("if the training does not improve
anymore, then cut off"). From 15M to 20M the empty-car wobble stayed at 20–30 deg/s and
empty-car push survival stayed at 0/18.

**Files:**

## Verdict

- **Loaded (2 kg): the best result so far.** It stands at 5–7 deg/s, at or below the PID
  level, and survives 3 N pushes 18/18. Best loaded checkpoints are about 16–17M.
- **Empty: no better than run 15.** It shakes at 20–30 deg/s from 15M on, and survives no
  pushes at all.
- **Interpretation:** the policy can't tell empty from loaded, so it settles on the stiff
  behaviour the load needs. The shake returns exactly when the payload and shoves reach
  full strength (about 15M).

## Firmware change needed for run 20 (laptop)

In `firmware/policy.c` `finish_obs()`, replace `obs[5] = obs[6] = 0`:
- Run the `load_adapt.c` filters once per 5 ms tick, with these inputs:
  - pitch gyro in deg/s (the same axis and sign as training's `gyro[1]`);
  - pitch angle in degrees, with all states zeroed when the policy arms.
- Then set:
  - `obs[5] = min(sqrt(E_osc) / 25, 4)`;
  - `obs[6] = min(sqrt(E_lean) / 2, 4)`.
- Reference implementation: `load_adapt_step()` in `train_real_robot.py`.
