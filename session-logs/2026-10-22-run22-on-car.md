## 3. Logging caveat

In long runs the D lines (speed, tilt) slowed from 10 Hz to about one per 0.8 s, while the P
stream kept going. Distances integrated from D-line speed are then rough.

The cause is not known; likely the UART is saturated by the P stream. For position checks
after pushes, either:
- log with the P stream off (`p`), or
- integrate the encoder columns of the P stream.

## 4. For the desktop

Run 22 is the best policy on the car so far: calm empty and loaded, slow creep with the right
trim, and it survives hand pushes. Open, in order:
1. **Empty 3 N pushes:** 0/18 in simulation for every run, against the PID's 17/18. Not
   measured on the car (no force gauge).
2. **Load-dependent trim:** 1.7° empty, about 2.5° with 2 kg. The policy could learn it if
   training randomised the payload's height or position as well as its mass, or the
   firmware could auto-trim slowly from the encoders (40 B of flash left; see
   `sim_tools/auto_trim.py` for the logic).
3. **Slope climbing with a load** (priority 3 in the run-22 log): run 21 with 2 kg fell at 5°.
